# Dinosaur Trumps art credits (scale-inset versions)

Every image is a modern life restoration (paleoart, not a fossil) from Wikimedia
Commons under a free licence (CC BY, CC BY-SA or CC0). Each file here is a resized
WebP derivative of the Commons original, with a scale panel added in the bottom-right
corner comparing the animal to a 1.8 m person: cropped to the animal and, where the
original had a transparent, white or black background, cut out onto transparency so
it sits on the card. Follow the file link for the full licence terms and the original.
