# Space Trumps picture credits

Every picture is from Wikimedia Commons under a free licence (public domain, CC0,
CC BY or CC BY-SA). Each file here is a resized WebP derivative of the Commons
original, cropped to the subject and, where the original sat on black, cut out onto
transparency so it sits on the card’s starfield. Follow the file link for the full
licence terms and the original.

Rows marked \* are artist’s impressions: Eris, Haumea and Makemake have never been
visited by a spacecraft, so no close-up photograph of them exists. Every other card
is a real photograph.
